Welcome to AG Harvesting Ltd.

Welcome to AG Harvesting Kenya Ltd, the leading agricultural contracting company based in Eldoret, Kenya. With a steadfast commitment to empowering farmers, we offer top-quality agricultural services to clients across Kenya and surrounding counties.

Our mission is to deliver cost-effective, reliable, and innovative farming solutions, making us the preferred partner for farmers seeking excellence in agricultural operations.

Our Philosophy

We pride ourselves on being "The Down to Earth Contractors." Our approach combines vast experience, advanced equipment, and a customer-centric attitude to provide superior agricultural solutions. Whether you're looking for complete farm services or specific operational support, we are here to meet your needs efficiently and competitively.

Our Services

  • Forage Harvesting: High-quality silage and hay solutions to support livestock nutrition.
  • Silage Baling: Efficient baling services to preserve forage for long-term use.
  • Hay Baling: Precision baling for high-density and high-quality hay.
  • Contract Spraying: Uniform application of pesticides and herbicides using state-of-the-art machinery.
  • Land Cultivation: Comprehensive land preparation, including ploughing, harrowing, and subsoiling.
  • Planting: Advanced planting techniques to ensure uniform crop emergence.
  • Agricultural Machinery Repairs: Expert repairs and maintenance for farm equipment.

Why Choose Us?

  • Comprehensive Service Offering: From land preparation to planting and harvesting, we cover all aspects of farming.
  • Cost-Effective Solutions: Outsourcing to AG Harvesting is more affordable than investing in expensive equipment.
  • Modern Machinery: Our fleet includes advanced and well-maintained agricultural machines to ensure efficiency and accuracy.
  • Experienced Team: With years of industry experience, our team is skilled, professional, and dedicated to delivering excellence.
  • Customer-Centric Approach: We tailor our services to suit the unique needs of every farmer.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and reliable agricultural service provider in Kenya, revolutionizing farming practices and fostering sustainable agricultural growth.

Our Mission

To support Kenyan farmers with innovative, reliable, and cost-effective agricultural solutions, enhancing productivity and ensuring food security.
