Your Best Partner for Silage Services in Kenya

Unrivaled Expertise in Silage

We understand that quality silage is key to thriving livestock. Our team of experts will guide you through every step of the silage-making process—from harvesting to baling—ensuring you get the most nutritious and long-lasting feed for your animals.

State-of-the-Art Machinery

AG HARVESTING KENYA LTD uses the latest forage harvesting and baling machines, designed to maximize efficiency and preserve up to 85% of the crop's nutritional value. Whether it's maize, Napier grass, or other forage crops, our machines deliver precise chopping, compression, and wrapping for the best silage quality.

Why Silage?

  • Consistent Feed Supply: Even when green fodder is unavailable.
  • High Nutritional Value: Packed with essential proteins, fiber, and energy for dairy and beef cattle.
  • Long Shelf Life: Properly baled silage lasts months, maintaining freshness and nutrients.
  • Cost Savings: Avoid high costs of feed during drought by preparing silage in advance.

What Sets AG HARVESTING Apart?

  1. Comprehensive Silage Education: We don’t just offer services; we empower farmers with knowledge.
  2. Customized Solutions: Whether you have a small or large-scale farm, we tailor our services to meet your specific needs.
  3. On-Farm Services: We bring our experts and machines directly to your farm, saving you time and effort.

Transform Your Farm with Silage

In Kenya’s unpredictable climate, silage is the solution to drought-proofing your farm. With AG HARVESTING KENYA LTD by your side, you’ll not only ensure your livestock thrives but also reduce fodder costs and increase milk or meat production.
