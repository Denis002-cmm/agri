Cultivation

Your Best Partner for Cultivation Services in Kenya

At AG Harvesting Kenya Ltd, we bring expertise and efficiency to your fields with our professional agricultural cultivation services. Using advanced machinery and skilled operators, we help you prepare your land for planting with precision, speed, and care. From chisel ploughing and disc harrowing to deep tillage and subsoiling, we ensure your farm is ready for a bountiful harvest.

Why Farmers Need Us

  • Access to Advanced Cultivation Machinery: Our modern equipment ensures optimal soil preparation with uniformity, precision, and efficiency.
  • Time-Saving Solutions: Let our experts handle the heavy work while you focus on other critical farming activities.
  • Expert Operators: Our team is trained to operate specialized machinery with precision, ensuring your soil is prepared to the highest standards.
  • Cost-Effective: Gain access to top-tier equipment and expertise at an affordable rate without the burden of ownership.
  • Comprehensive Cultivation Services: Tailored solutions for breaking hardpan layers, aerating soil, and more.

How Our Services Work

  1. Farm Assessment: Assessing your land to determine its needs.
  2. Customized Cultivation Plan: Tailored plans for your soil and crop type.
  3. Precision Cultivation: Advanced machinery ensures optimal land preparation.
  4. Post-Cultivation Evaluation: We offer recommendations for further steps.
