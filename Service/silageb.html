What is Silage Baling?

Silage baling is the process of compressing and wrapping nutrient-rich crops like maize, sorghum, and hybrid napier to preserve them as high-moisture fodder. This process protects the feed from oxygen exposure, ensuring it retains up to 85% of its nutrients. Properly baled silage serves as a vital source of feed during Kenya’s dry seasons or droughts, safeguarding your livestock’s health and productivity.

The Benefits of Silage

  • Year-Round Feed Supply: Silage ensures your livestock has access to quality feed, even during droughts or low-production seasons.
  • Improved Livestock Nutrition: With 85% nutrient preservation, silage promotes higher milk yields, better weight gain, and overall animal health.
  • Cost-Effective Solution: Silage is a more affordable alternative to purchasing green fodder during lean seasons.
  • Easy Storage & Longevity: Our compact silage bales are easy to store, require minimal space, and remain fresh for months.

Why Silage Matters

In a country prone to dry spells, silage is a game-changer. By preserving crops when they are most nutrient-rich, silage helps farmers combat feed shortages and avoid livestock losses. At AG Harvesting, we help you prepare for these challenges with modern baling technology and expert support.
