AG Harvesting Kenya Ltd

At AG Harvesting Kenya Ltd, we understand that well-maintained machinery is essential for efficient and profitable farming. That’s why we offer top-notch agricultural machinery repair services, tailored to keep your equipment running smoothly. From tractors and harvesters to milking machines and animal feed mixers, we have the expertise, tools, and facilities to handle all your repair needs.

Why Farmers Need AG Harvesting Kenya Ltd

  • State-of-the-Art Repair Workshop: Advanced tools and equipment for precise and efficient servicing.
  • Skilled Technicians: Certified professionals experienced in various machinery types.
  • Quick Turnaround: Minimal downtime so you can get back to farming fast.
  • Comprehensive Services: From routine maintenance to emergency repairs, we’ve got you covered.

The Benefits of Regular Machinery Repairs

  • Increased Lifespan: Extend the life of your equipment and save money.
  • Improved Efficiency: Maintain peak performance for better productivity.
  • Reduced Downtime: Prevent unexpected breakdowns.
  • Cost Savings: Avoid costly replacements.
  • Enhanced Safety: Reduce the risk of accidents.

How Our Services Work

  1. Initial Assessment: Diagnosis to identify and address machinery issues.
  2. Customized Repair Plan: Tailored solutions for mechanical, electrical, or hydraulic issues.
  3. Professional Repairs: High-quality parts and advanced tools ensure reliability.
  4. Testing and Quality Assurance: Rigorous testing before returning your equipment.
  5. Follow-Up Support: Maintenance tips and ongoing support for top performance.
