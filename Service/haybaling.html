Forage Harvesting

Your Best Partner for Silage Services in Kenya

At AG Harvesting Kenya Ltd, we bring efficiency and professionalism to hay baling, ensuring your forage is expertly handled and preserved for optimal quality. Whether you need square or round bales, our advanced machinery and skilled team provide reliable solutions tailored to your farm's needs.

Why Farmers Choose Us

  • State-of-the-Art Machinery: We use top-tier equipment to ensure high-quality bales.
  • Tailored Solutions: Custom services to suit your preferences.
  • Time and Cost Savings: Efficient service lets you focus on other tasks.
  • Complete Forage Handling: End-to-end management of hay and straw.
  • Quality Assurance: Preserve forage with minimal loss.

Benefits of Hay Baling

  • Enhanced forage quality for healthier livestock.
  • Convenient and easy-to-handle bales.
  • Reduced waste and spoilage.
  • Year-round feed supply for your animals.
  • Increased market value of high-quality bales.

How Our Services Work

  1. Initial Assessment: Inspect and plan for optimal baling.
  2. Mowing: Clean cuts with triple mower conditioners.
  3. Tedding: Aerate grass with wide Lely tedders.
  4. Baling: Create square or round bales with advanced machinery.
  5. Wrapping: Preserve forage with McHale 998 wrappers.
  6. Carting: Safely transport bales with loader tractors and trailers.

Our Services

  • Square Baling: High-density and market-specific bales.
  • Round Baling: Efficient baling and wrapping solutions.
  • Mowing: Precision mowing for optimal results.
  • Tedding: Aerate and dry grass effectively.
  • Bale Carting: Safe and efficient bale transportation.
