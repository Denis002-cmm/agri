Planting the Seeds of Success

At AG Harvesting Kenya Ltd, we bring innovation and expertise to your farm with our contract planting services. We help you achieve higher yields, lower costs, and efficient use of resources using advanced planting machinery and skilled professionals.

Why Farmers Need Us

  • Access to the Best Planting Machinery: Cutting-edge machines ensure precise seed placement and consistent growth.
  • Save Time and Labor Costs: Free up valuable time and avoid the hassle of managing labor.
  • Cost-Effective Solutions: Benefit from modern equipment without the upfront costs.
  • Enhanced Productivity: Maximize land use with precision planting techniques.

The Benefits of Contract Planting

  • Precision and uniform seed placement for better yields.
  • Reduced wastage saves you money.
  • Scalable services to meet your farm's size and needs.
  • Eco-friendly planting practices for sustainable farming.
  • Stress-free planting seasons managed by experts.

How Our Services Work

  1. Farm Assessment: Analyze your land, crop type, and planting needs.
  2. Customized Planting Plan: Tailored strategies for optimal results.
  3. Professional Planting: High-quality planting by skilled operators.
  4. Post-Planting Support: Ongoing advice for thriving crops.

Why Farmers Trust Us

  • Proven Expertise: Years of experience delivering reliable services.

  • Skilled Professionals: Trained teams ensuring precision and care.
  • Customer-Focused: Personalized solutions to meet your schedule and needs.
